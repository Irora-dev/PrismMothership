# Disclaimer

The Prism Mothership kit is open-source software that renders **public on-chain
data** about the PRISM token ecosystem. By deploying it you become the operator of
your own, independent instance.

- **Informational only.** Nothing this software displays is investment advice, an
  offer, or a solicitation. Revenue and burn figures track third-party on-chain
  activity; they vary and can be zero. Past figures predict nothing.
- **No custody, no added fees.** The software never holds funds, takes no cut,
  and adds no fee of its own. Where a screen involves a transaction — claiming
  fees your own wallet has already earned, or the optional native trade when the
  operator enables it — the transaction is built client-side against the
  canonical contracts and signed only by your own wallet. In `matcha` trading
  mode, trading links lead to independent third-party venues that the kit's
  authors do not operate.
